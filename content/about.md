# About

I am a Schmidt AI in Science Fellow at the University of Chicago, where I work with Arvind Murugan and William Irvine. My research draws from biophysics, soft matter, and statistical physics. I am interested in the molecular origins of adaptability in living systems, which I investigate through a combination of minimal physical models and machine learning approaches.

Before coming to Chicago, I completed a Ph.D. on polymer self-assembly in the MIT Physics Department under the supervision of Leonid Mirny and Michael Brenner. In 2027, I will start as an assistant professor in the Department of Physics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst.
