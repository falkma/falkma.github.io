# About

I am a Schmidt AI in Science Fellow at the University of Chicago, where I work with Arvind Murugan and William Irvine. My research draws from biophysics, soft matter, statistical physics, and scientific machine learning.

I am broadly interested in the molecular origins of adaptability: how collections of simple components can acquire, store, and use information to respond to changing environments. I approach these questions through a combination of minimal physical models and machine learning methods.

Before coming to Chicago, I completed my Ph.D. in the MIT Physics Department, advised by Leonid Mirny and Michael Brenner, where I studied polymer self-assembly. In 2027, I will start as an assistant professor in the Department of Physics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst.
