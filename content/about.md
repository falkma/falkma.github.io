# About

I am a Schmidt AI in Science Fellow at
the University of Chicago, working at the intersection of biophysics, soft matter, and statistical physics. My primary mentors are Arvind Murugan and William Irvine. I am interested in the molecular origins of adaptability in living systems, which I investigate through a combination of minimal physical models and machine learning approaches.

Prior to this, I completed a Ph.D. on polymer self-assembly in the MIT Physics Department under the supervision of Leonid Mirny and Michael Brenner. I will be starting a group as an assistant professor in the Physics department at the University of Massachusetts Amherst in 2027.
